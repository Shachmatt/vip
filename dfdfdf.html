⚙️ The science: from sand grains to living earth
1

Bind the Sand

Bacteria produce sticky biofilms → glue loose grains together → stops water from disappearing instantly

2

Capture Nitrogen from Air

Special bacteria convert atmospheric N₂ → plant-usable nutrients. Deserts have none, now we create fertility.

3

Pull Carbon from CO₂

Photosynthetic microbes (algae/cyanobacteria) turn CO₂ → organic sugars → first layer of "fake soil"

4

Build Organic Matter

Microbes die → decompose → form humus. Now you have something that resembles real soil.

5

Hold Water Like It's Precious

Our hydrogel absorbs & slowly releases moisture → critical for desert survival & plant resilience.

✅ Result: degraded sand → self-sustaining loam-like soil within months

Engineered microbial consortium

Specially selected nitrogen-fixers (Azospirillum, Rhizobium-like) + cyanobacteria (Nostoc, Anabaena) + biofilm-forming Bacillus strains. Works synergistically to regenerate soil health even in extreme arid climates.

Field pilot (UAE): After 90 days sand plots treated with LSS gel increased organic carbon by 340% and water holding capacity by 5x.

Scalable tech for moonshot impact

Application via agricultural sprayers, drones, or integrated with desert reforestation projects. Low energy, high efficiency — perfect for off-grid regions. Turns a planetary challenge into a regenerative economy.
